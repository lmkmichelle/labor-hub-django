Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information Labor Hub collects, how we use it, who receives it and the choices you have. See also our Terms of Use.

Information we collect

  • Application details: name, email address, position, department, affiliation, country, website, other research networks, your motivation for joining, a CV or resume, and, for scholars, two research papers. Students also name an advisor.
  • Profile details: photo, biography, research interests and newsletter and alert preferences.
  • Content you submit: discussion papers, job market papers, events, job postings, special issues, long-distance visits, and messages sent through the contact form.
  • Technical data: a session cookie and a security (CSRF) cookie, and the number of times each paper is downloaded.

What is public

Your name, position, affiliation, country, website, research interests, photo, email address and the content you post are shown publicly on your profile and on the site, by design, to anyone, whether or not they are signed in.

How we use your information

  • To review your application and run your account.
  • To email your advisor so they can confirm a student application. Your advisor sees your name and a review link.
  • To send the newsletter (weekly, monthly or off; you choose when you apply and can change it at any time in Settings) and any alerts you turn on.
  • To respond to messages you send us and to send account notices such as approvals.
  • To operate, secure and improve the site.

Email tracking

Newsletter and alert emails are sent through Campaign Monitor, which records whether and when emails are opened and links are clicked, so we can see which content is useful. Each email has an unsubscribe link, and you can switch emails off in Settings.

Who receives your information

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers:

  • Campaign Monitor: sends the newsletter and alerts (your email, name and email preferences).
  • Upsun: hosts the site, database and stored files.
  • Sentry: error monitoring, without user identity.
  • Cornell University, which hosts the Hub.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the security of the service.

Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies: one that keeps you signed in, one that protects forms from forgery, and one that shows one-time messages. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Sample papers you upload with an application are deleted when it is approved.
  • For rejected applications, uploaded files are deleted after 30 days; the record (name, email and answers) is kept.
  • Student accounts are deactivated two years after joining.
  • Accounts and their content stay until you delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Update your profile at any time.
  • Change or turn off emails in Settings.
  • Delete your account yourself from Settings. This removes your profile, photo, CV, applications and your subscription to our emails; papers and posts you made stay on the site with your name as plain text (not linked to an account).
  • For access to or correction of your data, or any other request, contact us.

Children

The Hub is for adults and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
