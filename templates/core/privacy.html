Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

This policy explains what information Labor Hub collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide, such as your name, email address, position, affiliation, country, biography, and research interests.
  • Content you submit, including discussion papers, events, job postings, visits, and messages sent through our contact form.
  • Preferences, such as your email digest frequency.

How we use your information

  • To display your profile and the content you contribute.
  • To send the email digests and notifications you opt into (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To respond to inquiries you send us.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Profile details and content you submit are shown publicly on the site by design. We may share information when required by law or to protect the security of the service.

Your choices

You can review and update your profile at any time, and change or turn off email digests from your profile's email preferences. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, please contact us.
