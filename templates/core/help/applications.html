Approving and denying new members

When someone applies to join Labor Hub, their application waits for an administrator to review it. No account exists and they cannot sign in until you approve them.

← Back to the admin guides

Reviewing an application

  1. Open the pending applications list.
  2. Each row shows the applicant's email, name, when they applied, and their résumé. To see everything they submitted — including their motivation and any research papers they attached — click their email address to open the full application.
  3. In the Approve/Deny Application column, click Approve or Deny.

There is no confirmation step. One click decides it, and the buttons disappear from that row afterwards. Read the application before you click.

What happens when you approve

  • Their account is created and activated immediately.
  • Their position, institution and country are copied across to their new profile.
  • They are emailed to say their account is live, with a link to sign in. They use the password they chose when they applied — you never see it and never need to send them one.
  • A green banner confirms "Application approved! User account created for …".

What happens when you deny

  • The application is marked Rejected. No account is created.
  • The applicant is told nothing. The site sends no rejection email, and because they have no account there is no way for them to check. If you want them to know, email them yourself — their address is in the Email column.
  • The banner is green and says "Application rejected". Green is just the site's standard confirmation colour; it does not mean you approved anyone.

Things worth knowing before you start

Denying is permanent

A rejected application cannot be reopened from this screen. If you deny someone by mistake, the way to fix it is to create their account by hand under Accounts → Users → Add user, or to ask them to apply again.

Student applications lose their advisor

Students name an advisor when they apply, but that choice is not carried over when you approve them, and it is not shown on the application screen either. If you are approving a student and the advisor link matters, set it afterwards: Accounts → Users → the student → Advisor.

"A user with this email already exists"

This red message means someone already has an account with that address — usually because it was created by hand, or because the person applied twice. The application stays pending, so nothing is lost. Check Accounts → Users for the existing account; if it is genuinely the same person, deny the duplicate application.

Approve and Deny only appear while an application is pending

Once reviewed, the column shows plain text such as Status: Approved. If you use a bookmarked or back-button link to a decision you have already made, you will see "This application has already been reviewed."

Deleting an application does not delete the member

After approval the account and the application are separate records. Deleting the application leaves the member's account working. To remove a member, delete them under Accounts → Users instead — or untick Active to suspend them without losing their content.
