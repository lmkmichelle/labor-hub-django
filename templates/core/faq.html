Frequently asked questions

Commonly asked questions about the platform. Can't find your answer? Get in touch.

Applications and accounts

How long does it take for an application to be processed?

Every application is read by an administrator, usually within 1–2 days, though it can sometimes take longer. If you are approved you will receive an email with a link to sign in, using the password you chose when you applied. Applicants who are not approved are not emailed, so if you haven't heard anything after 2 weeks, contact us.

I'm a student. How do I eventually get a fellow (researcher) profile?

Student memberships don't convert automatically. To become a fellow, you'll need to submit a formal application, which is reviewed like any new application: apply as a fellow. On the form, tell us you previously had a student account. If you apply with the same email, your student account is upgraded and everything you posted stays attached. If you use a different email, give us the number of your job market paper and, once you're approved, we'll attach it to your new account.

Student accounts are deactivated two years after they are created. We email you a month beforehand. Nothing you've posted is removed, and you don't need an account to read anything on Labor Hub.

Discussion papers

How long does it take for a paper to be processed?

Each submitted paper is individually processed by an administrator, usually within 2–3 days. Once the paper has been approved, it will be published on the platform.

Can I revise a discussion paper after it has been processed?

The PDF of a published paper is fixed and can't be replaced. You can still edit the other details, such as the title, abstract and topics, from the paper's page.

To share a revised version, submit it as a new paper and tick “Is this a revised version of a previous discussion paper?”, entering the number of the paper you are revising. Once approved, it is published with a version number: if the original is 323, the first revision is 323.1, the next 323.2, and so on. The original and its revisions are linked to each other under “Other Versions” on each paper's page.

Announcements: events, jobs and visits

How long does it take for an announcement to be displayed?

Workshops, conferences, jobs and long-distance visits are all reviewed by an administrator before they appear, usually within 1–2 days. They go live as soon as they are approved.

Alerts

Can I set up alerts for visits to specific universities?

Yes. Under Settings → Alerts, add the institutions you care about, and you'll get a weekly email when a visit to one of them is posted. You can also choose countries. Leave the alerts empty to turn them off.

Can I set up alerts for certain themes in discussion papers?

Yes. Under Settings → Alerts, pick the research topics you follow, and you'll get a weekly email when a new paper on one of them is posted.
