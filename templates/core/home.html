Labor Hub

Welcome to the Labor Hub! We are a global hub for the labor economics community. Our aim is to foster interactions and facilitate the sharing of information between researchers. At the Labor Hub, members can post job openings, advertise and learn about upcoming events, workshops or journal special issues, and coordinate research visits to initiate additional interactions during a sabbatical or long-distance travel. They can also share new working papers. All content is openly accessible.
