About Labor Hub

Labor Hub is an online community for researchers in labor economics. It brings together working papers, upcoming events and workshops, job openings for junior and senior researchers, and planned academic visits — alongside a directory of scholars and an interactive world map of research activity.

Our goal is to make it easier for researchers to share their work, discover relevant opportunities, and connect with colleagues around the world.

What you'll find here

  • Discussion papers — a searchable library of recent working papers.
  • Events — workshops and conferences you may want to attend or submit to.
  • Jobs — a board of academic openings across career stages.
  • Visits — sabbaticals and research visits that members plan to share.
  • Scholars — a directory of researchers and their interests.

Have a question or suggestion? We'd love to hear from you — please get in touch.
